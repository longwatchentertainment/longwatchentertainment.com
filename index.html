The studio

Behind the watchtower

Long Watch LLC is an independent game studio. Founder’s Plot grew out of a simple wish: to play a farming game where running the business was as interesting as growing the crop.

Choosing what to sell today, what to process, and when to pay for better equipment is the heart of the game we wanted to play.
