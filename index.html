Long Watch LLC is a one-person game studio in Kansas. We build agricultural and business-simulation games grounded in real economics.

Out now on iPhone and Android

Founder's Plot: Orchard Tycoon is a farm business game where you start with one apple tree and build a real orchard business, in real dollars. Grow and grade your apples, turn them into juice, jam, cider and more in the Processing Shed, then sell at a weekend farmers market where shoppers haggle over prices and send orders all week.

Free to play. Ads and purchases are optional.
